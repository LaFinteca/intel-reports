Visa restructures for the AI era.

The biggest payments company in the world just told the market where the next dollar goes: fewer people, more AI, and a product roadmap centered on stablecoins and cross-border.

EDITOR'S PICK GLOBAL RESTRUCTURING

Visa cuts 2,600 jobs as AI reshapes its product engine

Visa will eliminate roughly 2,600 roles, about 7% of its workforce, in a restructuring announced 28 July and effective 15 August. CEO Ryan McInerney is reallocating capital toward stablecoin and cross-border products, the two lines where fintech challengers have moved fastest. The cuts land on technology and product teams even as McInerney credits AI with a 65% boost in feature development velocity.

→ CNBC · 28 Jul 2026
Signal

When the incumbent with 60% card share funds its stablecoin and cross-border roadmap by cutting product headcount, it confirms the thesis: settlement infrastructure is where the margin war moves next, and lean regional operators get a window.

Global fintech, in four moves.

Patents, AI infrastructure, bank charters and B2B payments economics: the week's global stories all point at ownership of the underlying rails.

EDITOR'S PICK M&A

Circle buys IBM's blockchain patent vault

Circle acquired IBM's blockchain patent portfolio: more than 680 patent families and nearly 1,000 issued patents. The stablecoin issuer is arming itself for an IP phase of the infrastructure race, where owning the technology matters as much as shipping it.

→ Connecting the Dots · 28 Jul 2026
TALKS AI

Stripe reportedly in talks to acquire OpenRouter

Stripe is reportedly negotiating to buy OpenRouter, an AI model marketplace that routes requests across hundreds of models. After a decade building money-movement infrastructure, Stripe is positioning to own AI-usage billing infrastructure too.

→ Connecting the Dots · 27 Jul 2026
M&A BANKING

Stripe's first employee buys a bank

Darragh Buckley, founder of banking-as-a-service platform Increase, has acquired and rebranded a US bank to own his infrastructure outright. The new fintech playbook skips the sponsor-bank layer entirely and goes straight for the charter.

→ Connecting the Dots · 30 Jul 2026
B2B PAYMENTS

Sokin hits $90M RR net revenue on $12M raised

B2B payments company Sokin closed H1 2026 at $90M RR net revenue, up more than 100% year over year, and is tracking to $120M+. The striking part is efficiency: the growth sits on roughly $12M of primary capital, a counterexample to the burn-heavy cross-border cohort.

→ Connecting the Dots · 31 Jul 2026

LATAM in motion.

Mexico carries the week: Nu becomes a full bank, and Stake plants its poker flag ahead of the World Cup. Payments infrastructure keeps spreading south of it.

EDITOR'S PICK MEXICO LICENSING

Nu México goes full bank on 6 August

After a near three-year CNBV review, Nu México completes its SOFIPO conversion and begins operating as a bank on 6 August. David Vélez frames Mexico as a core bet with a projected $4.2B investment through 2030. Deposit ceilings and product limits fall away, and every PSP serving Mexican merchants inherits a stronger bank-grade competitor.

→ The Paypers · 30 Jul 2026
KEY ACCT MEXICO

Stake pushes deeper into Mexico with poker launch

Stake launched Stake Poker in Mexico, its first locally licensed market for the product, as its LATAM expansion accelerates ahead of the 2026 World Cup. Mexico is becoming Stake's regulated-market lab, which raises the stakes on local payment rails and payout speed.

→ Pokerfuse · Jul 2026
PRODUCT REGIONAL

Geopagos brings Tap to Pay on iPhone to LATAM

Acceptance-infrastructure provider Geopagos launched Tap to Pay on iPhone across Latin America, letting acquirers and fintechs turn iPhones into terminals with no extra hardware. Another step in the region's leapfrog past legacy POS fleets.

→ Latam Fintech Hub · 23 Jul 2026
EXPANSION COLOMBIA

DRUO preps Chile and Brazil after $160M processed

Colombian paytech DRUO is preparing entry into Chile and Brazil after processing $160M in payments in 2026. Direct bank-rail debits across markets is exactly the multi-country treasury pattern LATAM merchants keep asking for.

→ Latam Fintech Hub · 23 Jul 2026

Brazil up close.

Brasília had the pen out all week: the President backs stripping online casino from the Bets Law while the new advertising regime starts to bite. XP, meanwhile, keeps building its SMB ecosystem.

EDITOR'S PICK KEY ACCT REGULATION

Lula throws weight behind online casino ban

President Lula is backing PL 2,258/2026, the PT bill that would remove online casino games from the Bets Law while preserving regulated fixed-odds sports betting. With 78 licensed operators running 138 brands, a casino carve-out would hit the highest-margin vertical of nearly every key account in the market.

→ SBC News · 30 Jul 2026
KEY ACCT ADVERTISING

Betting ad rules bite, and the industry splits on them

Since 17 July every gambling ad in Brazil must carry a standardized Ministry of Finance addiction warning. Trade body ANJL backs the rules but demands matching enforcement against unlicensed platforms, while the lotteries association attacks the federal-municipal fragmentation of overlapping ad regimes.

→ Lottery Daily · 28 Jul 2026
PRODUCT SMB

XP launches payments terminal and card for SMBs

XP rolled out a payments terminal and business card for Brazilian SMBs, extending its corporate ecosystem beyond investments. Brazil's wealth platforms keep converging on payments, compressing acquiring margins for everyone already there.

→ Latam Fintech Hub · 22 Jul 2026
M&A ANTIFRAUD

Núclea buys antifraud platform Data Rudder

Clearing giant Núclea, which processes most of Brazil's card and boleto clearing, acquired antifraud platform Data Rudder to fold real-time fraud scoring into its infrastructure. Fraud tooling is consolidating into the rails themselves, a trend worth tracking for every PSP compliance stack.

→ Latam Fintech Hub · 22 Jul 2026

Crypto & stablecoins.

Institutional money enters through the front door at Crypto.com while $2B in USDT walks out of Revolut's. Stablecoin operations keep going mainstream on the B2B side.

EDITOR'S PICK FUNDING

Citadel Securities puts $400M into Crypto.com at $20B

Crypto.com landed its first institutional round in a decade of operating: $400M from Citadel Securities at a $20B valuation for a 2% stake. Market-making giants buying into retail crypto platforms says the institutional-retail wall is coming down.

→ Connecting the Dots · 20 Jul 2026
OUTFLOW STABLECOINS

USDT exodus at Revolut ahead of 31 August delisting

Users moved $855M of USDT off Revolut in one week and $2B over the month as the platform's 31 August delisting approaches. A live case study in how sticky stablecoin balances really are when a platform forces the choice.

→ Crypto Integrated · 30 Jul 2026
PRODUCT B2B

Ramp takes stablecoin accounts to general availability

Ramp made stablecoin accounts and payments generally available to all customers, one platform to hold, move and account for fiat and stablecoins together. Corporate finance teams now get stablecoin rails without leaving their spend platform.

→ PR Newswire · 21 Jul 2026
STABLECOINS INFRA

USA₮ goes live on Celo with day-one gas support

USA₮, the Anchorage-issued, Tether-supported US dollar stablecoin, launched on Celo with mint, burn and gas-currency support from day one. Celo's positioning as the payments-first stablecoin network keeps compounding, including across LATAM corridors.

→ Crypto Integrated · 30 Jul 2026
