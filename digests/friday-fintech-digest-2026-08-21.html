Brazil’s political class turns on its own regulated market

Eighteen months after Brazil licensed online betting, the President and the Governor of São Paulo spent this week arguing about how to shut it down. The enforcement machinery moved at the same time. For any PSP with Brazilian betting volume, this is the week the regulatory risk stopped being theoretical.

BRAZILREGULATION

Lula calls betting a scourge as the sector hands Brasilia R$7.3bn in six months

President Lula escalated his attack on licensed betting this week, saying “if no one can show me a social justification for these betting platforms to continue, we have to put an end to them.” The fiscal arithmetic cuts the other way: licensed operators paid nearly BRL 7.3 billion in federal taxes in H1 2026, against roughly BRL 10 billion across the whole of 2025, and Finance Minister Dario Durigan says more than five million Brazilians are already blocked from betting. The PT is pushing Bill PL 2,258/2026 to ban algorithm-based online casino games, targeting approval before the 4 October first round.

Both of Brazil’s likely 2026 presidential contenders are now campaigning against the sector that pays them. Tarcisio wants a total ban, Lula wants the casino verticals gone, and neither position needs the other to pass.

BRAZILPOLITICS

Sao Paulo governor: either Brazil ends the bets, or the bets end Brazil

Governor Tarcisio de Freitas told a SindHosp healthcare debate on 17 August that Brazil should ban online betting outright and fund a national gambling-addiction treatment programme. He is running for re-election in October, and his position goes further than the Lula government’s own bill, which targets only algorithm-based online casino games.

BRAZILDATA

Finance Ministry data puts 2025 betting deposits at R$220.6bn, with margin above the legal cap

Licensed operators took R$220.6 billion in deposits across 2025, returning R$183.7 billion to players and keeping R$36.9 billion in gross revenue. That implies a 16.7% take rate, above the 15% legal ceiling. October peaked at 10.82 million active players out of 25 million Brazilians who transacted during the year. Official Sigap figures came in R$130 billion below Comsefaz’s R$350.97 billion Pix-flow estimate, a gap ANJL attributes to illegal platforms.

Crypto and stablecoins: the licensing perimeter gets drawn

Treasury opened the first GENIUS Act rulemaking, the OCC promised final rules by November, and the SEC proposed its first dedicated crypto offering framework. Underneath the policy, Visa is shopping for a settlement partner and Chile quietly told everyone how stablecoin invoices are taxed.

REGULATIONSTABLECOIN

Treasury fires the starting gun on GENIUS Act stablecoin licensing

The US Treasury issued its first proposed rulemaking under the GENIUS Act on 17 August, defining who counts as a permitted payment stablecoin issuer under Section 3 and opening a 60-day comment period. From 18 January 2027 no one may issue a payment stablecoin in the US without a federal or state licence, and from 18 July 2028 digital asset service providers may not offer non-permitted stablecoins to US persons. Penalties run to $1m and five years.

Visa hunts a new stablecoin settlement partner after losing BVNK to Mastercard

Visa is running an RFP for a replacement settlement and OTC partner after Mastercard closed its BVNK acquisition on 3 August, a deal announced in March at up to $1.8bn. Per the RFP, Visa wants a firm that can settle multiple stablecoins including the new Open USD, holds exchange licences in the US, Canada, UK and Singapore, and matches BVNK’s 130-plus country footprint with 24/7 settlement. Visa reported a $7bn annualised stablecoin settlement run rate in early 2026.

EDITOR'S PICKCHILETAX

Chile rules stablecoin payments for cross-border services are payment in kind

Chile’s tax authority published Letter Ruling no. 1982 on 10 August, confirming stablecoins are not legal currency, foreign currency or divisas under Chilean law even when USD-pegged. A Chilean provider paid in stablecoins for a cross-border service is receiving a dacion en pago, payment in kind. Acquisition value equals the agreed service price and becomes the historical tax cost, with annual reporting via DDJJ 1963 and 1964 starting in Tax Year 2026.

REGULATIONUS

SEC proposes its first dedicated crypto offering rulebook

The SEC proposed Regulation Crypto Assets on 18 August, creating two Securities Act exemptions for covered investment contracts: a startup exemption up to $5m over four years and a fundraising exemption up to $75m per 12 months. It adds a conditional safe harbour letting an issuer delink a crypto asset from the investment contract it was sold with, plus state preemption. Comment period runs 60 days.

Gould also said the agency’s digital asset approval activity is running eightfold above the previous administration. The OCC separately gave conditional preliminary approval to World Liberty Trust as a national trust bank issuing USD1.

Brazil now prices its betting market as political risk.

The volume is holding and the tax take is real. Against that, two presidential-scale politicians are campaigning on shutting parts of the sector down, two state legislatures are moving on advertising, and the SPA is suspending licensed domains over Sigap failures. Anyone carrying Brazilian betting flow should be pricing operator concentration and refund-rail obligations into this quarter. The counterweight sits in the same digest. Betsson made LATAM its largest region at €112.1m, dLocal grew TPV 92%, and UnionPay is working to plug a foreign wallet straight into Pix. The region keeps compounding. Brazil is simply getting more expensive to read correctly.
