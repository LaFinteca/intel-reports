The story of the week.

Three of the largest names in cross-border money movement put stablecoins into production inside seven days. The settlement layer stopped being a pilot.

EDITOR'S PICKSTABLECOINSM&A

Mastercard closes BVNK and buys its way into the stablecoin stack

Mastercard completed its acquisition of stablecoin infrastructure provider BVNK, converting a year of strategic signalling into owned rails. The move gives Mastercard issuance, custody and payout plumbing it previously had to partner for, and it lands in the same week Western Union and Visa pushed their own stablecoin capabilities live.

→ Connecting the Dots in FinTech · 04 Aug 2026
PAYOUTSINFRASTRUCTURE

zerohash switches on stablecoin prefunding for Visa Direct

zerohash now powers stablecoin prefunding and payout capability for eligible Visa Direct clients, reaching a stated 18 billion endpoints across 195+ countries. Businesses can prefund in stablecoins and disburse through one of the largest payout networks in existence, collapsing a treasury step that used to require pre-positioned fiat.

→ Connecting the Dots in FinTech · 06 Aug 2026
Signal

Stablecoin settlement moved from proof-of-concept to owned infrastructure in a single week. The competitive question for PSPs is no longer whether to support stablecoin payout, but who owns the rail underneath it.

LATAM in motion.

Chile opens the payout question to consultation, orchestration capital lands in Colombia, and a licensed local-methods play compresses the coverage moat.

EDITOR'S PICKREGULATIONCHILE

Chile's CMF puts payout and payment initiation on the card-operator perimeter

The CMF opened a four-week public consultation on amendments to Annex 5 of Circular 1 for card operators, proposing to add three complementary activities: payment initiation services, TEF bank-transfer acceptance for affiliated merchants through payment buttons or terminals provided funds are not retained and a contract exists with the account issuer, and payout services tied to transactions the operator already processes. Consultation closes 9 September 2026.

→ CMF Chile · 12 Aug 2026

Why the CMF item matters

Chile is proposing to let card operators run payout and payment initiation inside their regulated perimeter, which changes who can legally hold the merchant payout relationship in one of LaFinteca's five primary markets. The consultation window closes 9 September.

Brazil up close.

The regulated betting market took its first serious political hit of the cycle, while credit fintechs kept raising against receivables.

EDITOR'S PICKREGULATIONBRAZIL

Lula and the PT put their weight behind an online casino ban

President Lula and the Workers' Party publicly backed PL 2,258/2026, filed 7 May by deputy Paulo Pimenta, which would preserve sports wagering while eliminating online casino games generated by electronic systems or algorithms. Running alongside it is PL 1,808/2026, filed 14 April by deputy Pedro Uczai with at least 68 PT lawmakers, which would ban all online gambling and repeal the framework entirely. Congress returned from winter recess on 3 August with the PT preparing to intensify, and the government wants movement before the October general election.

→ SBC News · 30 Jul 2026

Flutter's Q2 2026 showed Brazil revenue up 64% on the Betnacional acquisition, with integration milestones hit in both Brazil and Italy. Management flagged that operational progress was offset by a slowdown in the Brazilian online market driven by government socio-economic measures. Risk flag

Brazil is the only major LATAM market where an operator can hold a valid federal licence and still face repeal of the framework that issued it. Anyone underwriting Brazilian iGaming volume should be modelling the October election as a binary.
