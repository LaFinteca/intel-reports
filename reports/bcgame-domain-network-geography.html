Thirteen domains, one operator.

BC.Game runs a wide domain family beyond the flagship bc.game: link/redirect domains (bc.co, bc.app), ccTLD and country-skin domains (bcgame.sk, bcgamebet.com), a vertical brand (bcgameesports.com), an alt-brand (hash.game), a legacy alt-domain in decline (bcgame.com), and — the standout finding here — five domains that had zero measurable traffic before April 2026 and now carry the majority of the network's volume: playglobal3.com playglobal4.com playglobal5.com idplaywin.com wc2026-adu.pages.dev.

EU-27: thin, dispersed, no anchor market.

Estimated ~690K monthly visits from EU-27 across the network (≈34% of the 2.05M combined target-market total). No single EU country dominates — consistent with the patchwork of national restrictions on crypto-casino advertising across the bloc. bc.game's own EU-27 share sits at 11.1%, its most even geography of any target grouping, topped by Ireland, Germany and Belgium at ~1% each. bcgameesports.com is the outlier: 27.2% of its traffic is EU-27, led by Sweden, Germany and Poland — a genuinely different, EU-skewed esports audience rather than the casino core.

Brazil and Ukraine are the real story.

Brazil (568K/mo) and Ukraine (494K/mo) are the two largest markets in this set — bigger than the entire EU-27 in Ukraine's case, and close to it for Brazil. Mexico, Chile and Peru trail well behind. But the composition matters more than the totals: Brazil traffic is 93% mirror-wave, Ukraine is 90%, Mexico 74%, Chile 78%, Peru 71%. Five months ago none of that traffic existed on these domains at all.

The new domains don't engage like the flagship.

On bc.game itself, engagement in the target markets is real: Ukraine averages 9m36s and 15.8 pages per visit at a 20% bounce rate — the strongest engagement of any market in this set, EU-27 included (34.8% bounce). Brazil is the weak spot even on the flagship — 42 seconds average duration, 2.8 pages per visit. On the mirror-wave domains that pattern degrades further, and on wc2026-adu.pages.dev it disappears entirely.

Signal

0-to-3-second sessions and 81–100% bounce, one page per visit — that's not casino browsing, that's a redirect layer. wc2026-adu.pages.dev is hosted on Cloudflare Pages' free tier, a pattern typical of disposable geo-routing infrastructure, not a marketed consumer brand.

Why this matters for LaFinteca.

BC.Game is an offshore crypto casino operating outside licensed betting frameworks in most of these markets. Brazil's regulated sports-betting regime (Lei 14.790) does not cover this kind of crypto casino operator, and the mismatched-geography pattern on domains like bcgame.sk (Slovak ccTLD, zero Slovak traffic) plus the redirector signature on wc2026-adu.pages.dev point to active geo-blocking circumvention, not organic local expansion. That combination — unlicensed vertical, disposable mirror infrastructure, concentrated bursts into markets where the flagship is presumably restricted — is the textbook profile LaFinteca's compliance layer screens for, on both the PSP and Dindix sides.

Confidence & caveats

  • Country-level visit estimates — the uploaded SimilarWeb exports give traffic share by country over the full Aug 2025–Jul 2026 window, not month-by-month. I multiplied each domain's full-period country share by its July 2026 total visits (pulled live via the SimilarWeb API) to get a current run-rate estimate. Treat all "K/mo" and "M" figures in this report as directional, not exact.
  • Mirror-wave domains have thin history — playglobal3/4/5.com, idplaywin.com and wc2026-adu.pages.dev only have 3–4 active months of data. Their country-share percentages are less statistically stable than the flagship's 12-month figures.
  • wc2026-adu.pages.dev country ranks — SimilarWeb returned "N/A" for country rank on this domain in the raw export; treated as a data-coverage gap, not a zero.
  • EU-27 defined as the 27 current EU member states; UK, Norway, Switzerland excluded.
  • No independent verification was done on WHOIS, hosting, or corporate registration for the mirror-wave domains — the "geo-block circumvention" read is an inference from traffic pattern and engagement signature, not a confirmed technical finding.
