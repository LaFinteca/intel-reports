The headline is concentration. Brazino777 alone accounts for 1.29 billion of the 1.55 billion visits, and a single domain, brazino777.bet.br, carries 980 million of them. Everything else in this scan is an order of magnitude smaller. Read the four brand sections as four different business models.

Of the combined weighted traffic, 89.3% lands in Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Colombia or Peru, the five markets LaFinteca is live in. That is a comfortable number, but it hides two findings: the out-of-corridor remainder is dominated by one country, and a second is larger than v1.0 could see.

Signal

Argentina absorbs 66.7M visits at 4.3% of total volume. Ecuador, invisible until Pin-Up's unlisted mirrors surfaced, is 7.7M and uncontested, but section 10 shows how that traffic they get before treating it as a market.

How these numbers were built.

The uploaded exports contain country-level traffic share per domain. They leave out absolute visits. Share alone cannot be summed across domains: a domain with 100% Chile and 12,000 visits would otherwise weigh the same as one with 96% Chile and 87 million. To produce brand-level rankings, every domain was matched against the SimilarWeb API for its 12-month visit total, and each country share was multiplied by that total before aggregation.

Referral data was pulled per domain for the May–July 2026 window SimilarWeb. Coverage here is thin by design: SimilarWeb hides referral detail below a traffic threshold, so only 43 of 160 domains returned incoming referrals and 52 returned outgoing. The silent domains are all low-volume mirrors, so this is a coverage threshold at work. In v4.0 payment analysis moved off referrals entirely and onto technology detection, for reasons set out in section 08.

Data integrity flags

Three issues materially affect how these figures should be read, and none of them are cosmetic.

  • Pin-Up under-counting, resolved in v2.0. The v1.0 scan had five Pin-Up domains against 1win's 77 and flagged the total as a floor. A manually compiled mirror set added 17 valid domains and 10.4M visits, a 56% increase, and moved Ecuador from 13.2% to 25.1% of the brand. These mirrors are not linked from pin-up.world and were not findable through SimilarWeb's own competitor views. Treat the revised 28.9M as a floor.
  • Two 1win domains returned no data. 1win.casino and 1win.uy have zero country rows and zero visits in SimilarWeb. They are counted in the domain total but contribute nothing to the rankings.
  • Several volume spikes look bought. one-vv53243.com went from no data to 14.6M visits in a single month; 1wekkm.com jumped 566x between May and June 2026; jug-abet.com posted 9.3M and 9.5M in Nov and Dec 2025 against a 50K baseline either side. These are included in the totals but flagged in each brand section.

1win operates two distinct layers. The country-domain layer is 76 country domains, mostly dormant, functioning as protective registrations and market entry points. The mirror layer is a rotating set of 1w*.life, one-vv* and w-one* hosts that carry the actual sessions and are replaced continuously. The mirrors change 1win's LATAM shape in one specific way. Anyone reading 1win's Brazil position from its country domain alone would understate it by a factor of a hundred.

Counting caveat

Mirror rotation means one user can appear across several domains in a 12-month window. The 115.1M figure counts visits. The mirror layer inflates it relative to the other three brands, so treat it as visits only.

Payment stacks and traffic origin.

Detected payment technologies per operator, replacing the referral-based view in v3.0. Referral data could not see cashiers; technology detection can.

A method note first. Outgoing referrals proved to be the wrong instrument for payments. Across the 44 newly added 1win mirrors, zero payment destinations appeared, because mirrors redirect into 1win.com and the cashier sits behind login where click-tracking cannot follow. Technology detection reads the checkout directly and is used throughout this section.

Shared dependencies

AstroPay is the cross-brand LATAM rail. It is installed on Brazino777's Brazil, Mexico and Argentina properties and on 1win's Chile property, and was previously on 1win.com.br. No other alternative method spans two operator groups this way. OXXO is the second, shared between Brazino777 Mexico and 1win Brazil. Visa and MasterCard are universal and carry no competitive information.

The anti-fraud layer splits the field cleanly into two vendor camps. DataDome protects Pin-Up and Jugabet (mexico-pin-up.mx, pin-up.kz, jugabet.cl, jugabet.mx). 1win uses Cloudflare Bot Management with ThreatMetrix. Brazino777 sits apart again with Fraudblocker and hCaptcha, both now removed. That is a clear marker of which operators share procurement.

Signal

Pix is installed on Pin-Up's Mexican and Ecuadorian domains and absent from 1win's Brazilian one. Both are wrong for their market, and both point to shared checkout templates deployed without market-level gating.

Where the traffic comes from

The referral picture from v3.0 still holds for acquisition, and the 45 new mirrors make it clearer. Brazino777 and Jugabet buy adult traffic at scale: brazino777.bet.br takes 68.7% of referrals from xvideos.com, brazino777.com takes 70.8% from es.pornhub.com, jugabet.cl takes 39.1% combined from erome and xvideos properties. Pin-Up's Ecuador property runs on piracy, covered in section 10.

1win's newly visible mirror chain shows a third model. The 1w*.life, one-vv* and w-one* domains exist only to forward users onward, and their own inbound traffic is bought from low-quality inventory: hosts that look like ad-based malware (masterlythehague.com, interlinecustomroofingllc.com, spendsdetachment.com), adult sites, PTC and faucet networks (wmrfast.com, surfearner.com, litefaucets.com), and sports piracy (watch-livefootball.tv at 87.9% of r1wxdpc.life). Regional entry points are cleaner: 1wmzsk.life is fed 95.3% by 1wins.cl for Chile, r1walrc.life by 1win-apuestas.pe for Peru, 1wqxun.life by Brazilian affiliates 333bet.com.br and 456bet.com.br.

Where they advertise

SimilarWeb's keyword exports carry no country dimension, and the country-filtered keyword endpoint returns no data for these domains, so advertising geography has to be worked out from keyword language and market-specific brand names. On that basis the answer is clear for Brazino777.

Brazino777's paid search is effectively all Brazil. Of 950,820 paid clicks across 975 paid keywords, 89.8% are Portuguese-language or Brazilian-brand terms. The remaining 10.2% is not a second market: it is Brazilian operators whose names do not contain a Portuguese word, novibet, r7 bet, 7kbet, 522bet, bc game. Every ad in the July 2026 set is Portuguese, carries the Brazilian licence marker SPA/MF Nº 466/2025, and lands on a .bet.br domain. There is no detectable paid activity outside Brazil, which matters because brazino777.mx runs 266.8M visits with no paid search behind it.

This is a brand that has already won its market and is simply collecting the traffic. Confidence & caveats

  • Country rankings — calculated by us. Uploaded exports gave share only; visit totals came from the SimilarWeb API and were multiplied through. Any error in a domain's visit estimate carries through to its country figures.
  • Pin-Up is now 22 domains, still incomplete. 17 mirrors supplied manually were validated and integrated; two supplied domains were rejected as third-party (pinup.en.uptodown.com, an Uptodown app page, and casino.pin-pinup.radio.fm, a radio.fm piggyback subdomain). Because these mirrors are unlinked and impossible to find automatically, further domains almost certainly exist. Treat 28.9M as a revised floor.
  • Referral coverage is 20% incoming, 25% outgoing. SimilarWeb hides referral data below a volume threshold. When a domain has no referral data, it means the traffic sits below the threshold. Note also that payment redirects often happen server-side or inside an iframe, where click-based referral measurement cannot see them. Section 08 gives a partial view of the payment stack.
  • Three traffic patterns are flagged as suspect — one-vv53243.com (no data to 14.6M in one month), 1wekkm.com (566x MoM), jug-abet.com (two 9M+ months against a 50K baseline). All are included in totals. Excluding them would cut 1win by roughly 28% and Jugabet's Argentina position by most of its value.
  • 1win.casino and 1win.uy returned no data. Counted in the 77-domain total, excluded from all traffic figures.
  • LATAM defined as 21 countries — the standard Latin America set including the Caribbean Spanish-speaking states. LaFinteca corridors per current scope: Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Peru, Chile.
  • Period — Aug 2025 to Jul 2026 for traffic, geography and keywords. May to Jul 2026 for referrals and Jul 2026 for search ads, both chosen for recency over comparability with the traffic window.
  • Keyword and ad data — supplied as SimilarWeb manual exports for brazino777.bet.br and jugabet.cl only. The other two brands have no keyword coverage in this report, so the acquisition contrast in section 09 should not be generalised to 1win or Pin-Up without equivalent pulls.
  • Search-click volumes below 50 are masked by SimilarWeb as "<50" in the ads export. Traffic-share percentages are used instead where that hiding applies. Referral traffic below 5,000 is hidden in the same way as "<5,000" in the Ecuador referral file, so we use shares there.
  • The Pix reading in section 08 is our interpretation. SimilarWeb technographics detects a technology signature on the page; it does not confirm the method is live, enabled for Ecuadorian users, or processing volume. The shared-template explanation fits the shared 4 Aug 2025 install date but has not been verified against the live checkout. Confirming it needs a manual deposit-flow inspection.
  • Absence of a detected PSP is weak evidence. Technographics reads client-side signatures. A server-side or iframe-hosted local rail would not appear. "No local Ecuadorian PSP detected" means this method cannot see one. A local rail may still be there.
  • 1win domain count is inflated relative to the other brands. 121 domains includes a rotating mirror layer where one user can appear across several hosts in a 12-month window. 115.1M counts visits. Brazino777, Jugabet and Pin-Up do not run comparable rotation, so cross-brand visit comparisons favour 1win.
  • 1xlite-82970.pro was excluded from the 1win set. The 1xlite- prefix is 1xBet's mirror scheme, its audience is Indonesia and Turkey with no overlap with any 1win property, and it stopped serving in Oct 2025. Including it would have added 4.31M visits from a different operator.
  • Payment data comes from technology detection. Outgoing referrals returned zero payment destinations across all 44 new 1win mirrors because the cashier sits behind login. Technographics reads client-side signatures on the page, so a server-side or iframe-hosted rail still would not appear. "Not detected" means not visible to this method.
  • 1win's payment stack has gone dark. Across 1win.lat, 1win.in, 1win.ng and 1win.mx every method is marked removed during 2025 and 2026. That points to a cashier rendered dynamically, which the crawler can no longer read.
  • Advertising geography is our interpretation. SimilarWeb's keyword exports carry no country column and the country-filtered keyword endpoint returns no data for these domains. The Brazil conclusion for Brazino777 rests on keyword language and Brazilian brand names, plus Portuguese ad copy carrying a Brazilian licence marker. It is a strong interpretation drawn from the evidence, and SimilarWeb does not report it directly.
  • Per-country PSP attribution was attempted and failed. The outgoing-referrals endpoint rejects a country parameter for every domain tested, including control domains, so we report payment methods per domain, and the market column reflects that domain's own audience.
  • Keyword coverage now spans four domains — jugabet.cl, brazino777.bet.br, pin-up.world and casino-pin-up.ec. 1win has no keyword data in this report and the acquisition contrast should not be extended to it.

What this means for LaFinteca.

Our five corridors cover 89.3% of the traffic these four operators generate, so geography is not the opening. Argentina is the only unserved market with scale across all four brands. Ecuador looked like the second opening until the technology stack explained it: 3.74M visits acquired almost entirely from piracy and pirated-sports referrers, running on Visa, MasterCard and what appears to be Brazilian Pix code carried over from another market with no local rail detected. The lasting finding is structural. Payku is the shared Chilean supplier for both 1win and Pin-Up. Brazino has internalised its rail through brazopay and will resist replacement. 1win spreads across five or more rails per market, which is the profile of an operator that adds providers on top of what it has. Approach them first. Across all four, referral and affiliate traffic dwarfs search. The pitch is settlement speed and domestic collection.
